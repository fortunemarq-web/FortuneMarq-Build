// FAQ

Questions fertility clinic owners usually ask us first

With only 350 monthly searches, is digital marketing worth it for IVF clinics? ▾
Absolutely. At ₹1.5L–3L+ per treatment cycle, even 5 consultations per month from digital marketing represents ₹7.5L–15L in potential revenue. The ROI is among the highest of any niche we work in.
How do you handle the sensitivity of IVF advertising? ▾
We build trust-first campaigns — doctor credentials, clinic facilities, patient testimonials (with consent), and empathetic ad copy. We never use sensationalist or exploitative messaging, and we comply fully with Google and Meta healthcare ad policies.
Can IVF clinics run Google Ads? ▾
Yes, with proper certification. Google requires healthcare advertisers to complete a certification process in India. We handle this on your behalf and manage compliant campaigns throughout.
How important is SEO vs Ads for fertility clinics? ▾
Both are critical. SEO builds long-term trust and organic visibility. Ads capture couples in active search mode. Together they ensure your clinic is visible at every stage of the patient's decision journey.
What if I want to stop after a few months? ▾
No lock-in. Retainers can be paused or stopped with 30 days' notice. All accounts and content are fully yours.
