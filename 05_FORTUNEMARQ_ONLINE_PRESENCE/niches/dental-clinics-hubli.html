How much does digital marketing cost for a dental clinic? ▾
It depends on your starting point. A clinic with no website needs different work than one already ranking on Google. Our dental retainers start from ₹7,000/month for SEO, with Google Ads management billed separately (plus your ad budget). We give an exact quote after the free audit — no surprises.
How quickly will patients start calling? ▾
Google Ads typically brings calls within 7–10 days. SEO takes 3–6 months to show consistent ranking changes — but those rankings are compounding and free. For immediate + long-term results, we recommend running both simultaneously.
My clinic already has a Google listing — do I still need help? ▾
Having a listing and having an optimised listing are completely different. Most dental clinic GMB profiles in Hubli are missing correct categories, services, photo strategy, and review systems. Our audit will show you exactly what gaps are costing you patients.
Do I need a website to run Google Ads? ▾
Yes — for search ads you need a landing page to send traffic to. We can build and launch your clinic website in 14–21 days, then start ads immediately after. We handle the full sequence so there's no delay.
What if I want to stop after a few months? ▾
No lock-in. Monthly retainers can be paused or stopped with 30 days' notice. Your website, Google accounts, and ad campaigns are fully yours — we never hold access.
