# GMB Reviews — requests + replies

Track review requests and reply to every review by hand (until the API is in place). Keep replies
plain, warm, and specific — thank by name, mention what they came for. No canned spam.

## Review-request tracking
| Date asked | Client / customer | Channel (WhatsApp/in-person) | Left a review? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

> Ask steadily (2–3/week), not in bulk — sudden spikes look fake to Google.

## Reply templates (personalise each one)
- **5★, named:** "Thank you, {name}! Really glad we could help with {what}. — Team FortuneMarq"
- **Positive, brief:** "Appreciate the kind words, {name} — thanks for trusting us."
- **Negative / critical:** Stay calm and professional, apologise for the experience, move it to a call:
  "Sorry to hear this, {name}. We'd like to make it right — please reach us at +91 93530 82656."

## Rules
- Never buy/fake reviews, never offer incentives for reviews (against Google policy).
- Reply to negatives quickly and take the detail offline.
