# FortuneMarq — PDF Index
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-15
**Purpose:** Maps every Market Intelligence PDF to its city, niche, type, language, and filename. Used by FMOS to automatically assign the correct PDF to each lead based on their niche, city, and SERP status.

---

## How PDFs Are Assigned to Leads

| Lead Segment | PDF Type | When to Send |
|---|---|---|
| SERP_Ranked = Y | Type 1 — Visibility Report | Lead already appears on Google Page 1 |
| SERP_Ranked = N, Has Website = N | Type 2 — Market Opportunity Report | Lead has no website and not on Google |
| SERP_Ranked = N, Has Website = Y | Type 3 — Website Performance Report | Lead has website but not ranking on Google |
| Any niche with volume under 2,500/month | Type 4 — Niche Market Report | Low search volume niche |
