How to rent a car for the lowest price?

AI Overview
To rent a car for the lowest price,
compare multiple sites, book in advance (but be flexible with dates), choose smaller, off-airport locations, and consider longer-term or one-way rentals if it lowers the daily rate
, plus look for deals on smaller cars or those with full-to-full fuel policies for maximum savings.
Booking Strategies
Compare Aggregators:
Use sites like Kayak, Skyscanner, or DiscoverCars to compare prices across many companies at once.
Book Early & Monitor:
Book a non-prepaid rate in advance, then use services like Auto Slash to automatically rebook if prices drop.
Be Flexible with Dates:
Check different days or months;
off-peak seasons (like June in India) are often cheaper.
Consider Longer Rentals:
Sometimes a 7-day rental is cheaper than 6 days due to weekly discounts.
Play with Locations:
Airport locations are convenient but pricier;
check nearby city branches or even one-way options for better deals.
Vehicle & Policy Choices
Right-Size Your Vehicle:
A compact car is cheaper than an SUV;
choose based on your actual needs.
Full-to-Full Fuel Policy:
This saves money by letting you refill the tank yourself, avoiding expensive company charges.
Unlimited Mileage:
Look for deals with unlimited mileage to prevent extra fees.
Hidden Costs to Watch For
Airport Fees
:
Airport pick-ups often have surcharges.
Insurance & Fuel
:
Understand insurance options and fuel policies to avoid overpaying.
Extra Hours
:
Be precise with pickup/dropoff times;
even an extra hour can cost a full day.
