Physiotherapy costs vary widely by location and specialization, but generally range from around
₹500 to ₹2,500 ($7-$30 USD)
for a 1-hour session in places like India, with home visits often costing more (₹800-₹2,000+) due to travel, and specialized/neurological care at the higher end.
Factors like the therapist's experience, equipment used, and the severity of your condition also influence the price.

Highly experienced or specialized therapists charge more.
Treatment Type:
Neurological or complex rehab is pricier than general mobility.
Equipment:

Is physiotherapist a MBBS doctor?
An AI Overview is not available for this search
Can't generate an AI overview right now. Try again later.
AI Overview
No, a physiotherapist is not an MBBS doctor
;
they are distinct healthcare professionals, with MBBS focusing on general medicine (diagnosis, surgery, drugs) and physiotherapy (BPT/MPT) focusing on movement, rehabilitation, and non-drug therapies, though they often work together, and some advanced physiotherapists with PhDs might use the "Dr."
title academically, but not as an MBBS physician.
MBBS doctors are medical doctors (allopathic), while physiotherapists are allied health professionals trained in physical rehabilitation.
Key Differences:
Training:
MBBS involves comprehensive medical training (5.5 years plus internships) for diagnosing and treating diseases, whereas physiotherapy education (like a Bachelor of Physiotherapy - BPT) focuses on musculoskeletal, neurological, and movement disorders.
Scope of Practice:
MBBS doctors can prescribe medication and perform surgery;
physiotherapists use exercise, manual therapy, and modalities for movement restoration and pain relief but cannot prescribe medicines or perform surgery.
Title:
An MBBS holder is a Medical Doctor.
Physiotherapists, particularly those with advanced doctorates (Ph.D.), may use "Dr."
academically, but they are not MBBS doctors, and regulatory bodies advise against using the prefix to avoid confusion, according to
NDTV
and
Careers360
.
Collaboration:
MBBS doctors and physiotherapists often work as a team, with the doctor diagnosing medical conditions and the physiotherapist managing the physical recovery and functional aspects, notes
V Care At Home
and Swaasa.

How much is the price for physiotherapy?
An AI Overview is not available for this search
Can't generate an AI overview right now. Try again later.
AI Overview
Physiotherapy prices vary widely by location, provider, and treatment type, but generally range from
₹500 to ₹3,000 INR per session
in India for clinic visits, with home visits often costing more (₹800-₹2,000+).
Expect higher fees for experienced therapists, specialized treatments (like manual therapy or neurological rehab), advanced equipment use, or premium clinics, while packages or longer-term plans usually offer discounts.
Typical Costs (India):
Clinic Session:
₹500 – ₹2,500+
Home Visit:
₹800 – ₹2,000+ (often higher than clinic)
Packages:
Discounted rates for multiple sessions (e.g., 10 sessions for ₹6,000–₹7,000) or monthly plans.
Factors Influencing Price:
Experience & Specialization:
Highly qualified or specialized physiotherapists (e.g., neurological, sports) charge more.
Treatment Type:
Manual therapy or advanced modalities (like ultrasound, TENS) cost more than basic exercise therapy.
Location:
Major cities often have higher rates.
Service Type:
Home visits add travel costs, making them pricier.
Equipment:
Use of specialized equipment increases costs.
How to Get a Quote:
Contact local clinics or home physiotherapy providers in your area (like those in Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad) and ask about their rates for your specific condition and treatment plan.
