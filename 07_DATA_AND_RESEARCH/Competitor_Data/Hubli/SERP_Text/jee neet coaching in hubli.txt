Key Considerations When Choosing:
Learning Style:
Do you prefer intensive, residential-style coaching (Allen) or more flexible, city-based classes (Aakash)?
Exam Focus:
Some institutes excel more in JEE (FIITJEE) while others are strong in both (Allen, Aakash).
Location & Online:

Look for experienced faculty and smaller batches for personalized attention.
