# 03 — Proposals
**Last Updated:** April 2026 | **Status:** COMPLETE — 5-6 page dynamic proposal + 1-page agreement. JSON schema + services data + TypeScript loader in FMOS_Proposal_Data/

## Purpose
The proposal is a dynamic PDF generated by FMOS after Jabeer meets with a prospect. It is the closing document that converts a meeting into a signed client. The agreement is a separate 1-page document confirming scope, pricing, and terms — sent via WhatsApp or email, confirmed by client reply.

## How It Works
1. Jabeer opens lead profile in FMOS after meeting
2. Selects services from service cards (checkboxes)
3. Enters pricing manually per service (not fixed — depends on client potential)
4. FMOS auto-fills: business name, owner name, city, niche, search volume, lead type, date, proposal number
5. FMOS generates 5-6 page proposal PDF
6. Jabeer sends PDF within 24 hours via WhatsApp

## Proposal Structure (5-6 Pages)

**Page 1 — Cover**
Business name, city, date, proposal number, prepared by Jabeer. FortuneMarq branding. Clean, professional.

**Page 2 — About FortuneMarq + The Online Growth System**
Short intro to FortuneMarq (data-backed, systems-driven, Hubli-based). System diagram: Foundation (Website/GMB) → Visibility (SEO/Ads) → Engagement (WhatsApp/AI). Not just ads — one connected system.

**Page 3 — Your Market Opportunity**
Type-specific content auto-filled from lead profile:
- Type A (ranked): "You're already ranking. Here's how we lock it and convert more."
- Type B (website, not ranked): "You have a website but you're invisible. Here's the gap."
- Type C (no website): "Your customers are searching. You're not showing up at all."
- Type D (low volume): "Your market is smaller. Here's how we dominate the niche with precision."

**Page 4 — Your Recommended Plan**
Service cards for selected services only. Each card: service name, what it includes, timeline, what the client gets. Framed as a system, not individual services.

**Page 5 — Investment**
Table: service, setup fee, monthly fee, timeline. Total setup investment. Total monthly retainer. Payment terms (invoice 1st, due 5th). Advance before work begins.

**Page 6 — Next Steps**
Agreement confirmation (client replies "Confirmed" or "Yes" to confirm). Work starts within 48 hours of payment. Contact info. Jabeer's direct number.

## Closing Sequence (Locked)
Proposal → Agreement Document → Client confirms by WhatsApp/email reply → Invoice → Onboarding starts

## Agreement Document
- 1-page document (not a full legal contract)
- Covers: services selected, deliverables, timeline, pricing, payment terms
- Sent via WhatsApp or email after proposal is accepted
- Client confirms by replying "Confirmed" or similar — no wet signature needed
- Linked to proposal by reference number
- Files: `agreement_template.json` (structure), `index.ts` (loader)

## Proposal Data Files for Code
Location: `Proposals/FMOS_Proposal_Data/`
- `proposal_schema.json` — Full 5-6 page structure with all sections and variables
- `services_data.json` — All 7 services with descriptions, deliverables, timelines, and pricing tiers
- `agreement_template.json` — Agreement document structure with all sections
- `proposal.types.ts` — TypeScript interfaces for proposal + agreement data
- `index.ts` — Loader utilities for both proposal and agreement

## Key Decisions (locked)
- Proposal = Quotation. One document does both jobs. No separate quotation doc.
- Agreement is a separate document — not just a WhatsApp reply. Client confirms by reply (no wet signature).
- Pricing entered manually by Jabeer. Not fixed in system — depends on client's business size and potential.
- 5-6 pages. Not 8 variants — one dynamic template that adapts to selected services and lead type.
- Proposal sent within 24 hours of meeting.
