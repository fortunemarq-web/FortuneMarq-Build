## Folder Purpose
The proposal is a dynamic PDF generated by FMOS after Jabeer meets with a prospect. It is the closing document that converts a meeting into a signed client. The agreement is a 1-page document confirming scope, pricing, and terms — sent via WhatsApp or email.

## Proposal Structure (5–6 Pages)

**Page 1 — Cover**
Business name, city, date, proposal number, prepared by Jabeer. FortuneMarq branding.

**Page 2 — About FortuneMarq + The Online Growth System**
Short FortuneMarq intro. System diagram: Foundation (Website/GMB) → Visibility (SEO/Ads) → Engagement (WhatsApp/AI).

**Page 3 — Your Market Opportunity**
Type-specific content auto-filled from lead profile:
- Type A (ranked): "You're already ranking. Here's how we lock it and convert more."
- Type B (website, not ranked): "You have a website but you're invisible. Here's the gap."
- Type C (no website): "Your customers are searching. You're not showing up at all."
- Type D (low volume): "Your market is smaller. Here's how we dominate the niche with precision."

**Page 4 — Your Recommended Plan**
Service cards for selected services only. Each: service name, what it includes, timeline, deliverables.

**Page 5 — Investment**
Table: service, setup fee, monthly fee, timeline. Total setup investment. Total monthly retainer. Payment terms.

**Page 6 — Next Steps**
Agreement confirmation. Work starts within 48 hours of payment. Contact info.

## How Proposals Are Generated (in FMOS Phase D)
1. Jabeer opens lead profile in FMOS after meeting
2. Selects services from service cards (checkboxes)
3. Enters pricing manually per service (no fixed rates — Jabeer decides per prospect)
4. FMOS auto-fills: business name, city, niche, search volume, lead type, date, proposal number
5. FMOS generates 5–6 page PDF
6. Jabeer sends PDF via WhatsApp within 24 hours
