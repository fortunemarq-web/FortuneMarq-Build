## 2. The Vision

FortuneMarq is not being built as a typical digital marketing agency.
It is being built as a **systems-driven, automation-first agency** where:

- Every service is backed by real data (252 Market Intelligence PDFs, 8,000 scraped leads, keyword research across 9 cities × 14 niches)
- Every repeatable task is automated — humans supervise systems, not execute tasks
- Every part of the operation lives in one tool (FMOS) — no spreadsheets, no external trackers, no WhatsApp to manage work
- Volume is the model — accessible prices, fast delivery via automation, strong portfolio, then price increases

**The end state (2 years):**
- ₹5,00,000/month revenue
- 5–10 person team
- All tier-1 cities of Karnataka covered
- Expansion into Hindi/English speaking states
- SaaS product live (Admissions CRM for coaching institutes)
- FortuneMarq is the most recognised digital marketing agency brand in Karnataka

**The principle that governs every decision:**
Everything being built — the CRM, the automations, the sales system, the delivery workflows, the content, the ads — is one interconnected machine. No folder in this build system is isolated. A decision in one folder affects others. Always think in context of the full system.

## 5. The Master Flow

This is how a lead becomes a client and how a client generates recurring revenue.
Every step is owned by a specific folder in this build system.

```
STEP 1 — DATA EXISTS
  Folder: 07_DATA_AND_RESEARCH
  8,000 GBP leads cleaned + sorted by city and niche
  Keyword data and competitor data ready per niche+city
  252 Market Intelligence PDFs ready to deploy

STEP 2 — CAMPAIGN RUNS
  Folder: 06_PAID_MARKETING
  Meta/Google ad launches for specific niche + city
  Founder video explaining real search data
  Traffic → niche+city landing page → lead captured → enters FMOS

STEP 3 — LEAD IN SYSTEM
  Folder: 01_CRM_AND_TOOL
  Lead auto-tagged: niche, city, source, assigned to Afifa
  Status: New → queued for 3-touch outreach

STEP 4 — OUTREACH SEQUENCE
  Folder: 03_SALES_SYSTEM
  Touch 1: WhatsApp curiosity message (Afifa)
  Touch 2: PDF report delivered (niche+city specific)
  Touch 3: Follow-up call → goal is booking meeting with Jabeer
  All outcomes logged in FMOS

STEP 5 — MEETING & CLOSE
  Folder: 03_SALES_SYSTEM
  Jabeer runs 15–20 min meeting
  Proposal sent within 24 hours
  Agreement signed → project created in FMOS

STEP 6 — INVOICE RAISED
  Folder: 08_FINANCE
  Invoice auto-generated in FMOS
  GST invoice sent via WhatsApp/email
  Advance payment collected before work starts

STEP 7 — ONBOARDING
  Folder: 04_CLIENT_MANAGEMENT
  Onboarding checklist triggered in FMOS
  Assets collected (logo, content, domain, hosting)
  Stored in client's Asset Vault in FMOS

STEP 8 — DELIVERY
  Folder: 02_SERVICE_DELIVERY_AUTOMATION
  Website: Brief App generates PRD + prompts → cousins build → Jabeer reviews → live
  SEO: Automation handles 70–80% of tasks → monthly report auto-generated
  Ads: Campaigns managed → performance monitored → auto-alerts on drops

STEP 9 — MONTHLY REPORT & INVOICE
  Folder: 08_FINANCE + 02_SERVICE_DELIVERY_AUTOMATION
  Auto-generated PDF report sent to client on 1st of month
  Monthly invoice raised and sent
  Payment tracked in FMOS

STEP 10 — RETAIN & UPSELL
  Folder: 04_CLIENT_MANAGEMENT
  Client health score monitored
  Upsell conversation triggered based on results data
  Renewal alerts 30 days before contract end

STEP 11 — BRAND BUILDS TRUST (PARALLEL)
  Folder: 05_FORTUNEMARQ_ONLINE_PRESENCE
  Instagram content running — niche data reels, behind the system, results
  GMB optimised — appearing in Hubli digital marketing searches
  SEO building — fortunemarq.com ranking for local agency keywords
  This feeds back to STEP 2 — inbound leads increase over time
```

### How to Start a Session
Open Claude Code (terminal) in the specific folder you're working in.
Start with: **"Read CONTEXT.md and continue."**
Claude will have full context of both that folder AND the master system.

### How to End a Session
Before closing, say: **"Update CONTEXT.md with everything we decided today."**
Claude updates the file. Next session picks up exactly where you left off.

### The Golden Rule
Every decision made in any folder must be considered in context of the full system.
If a decision affects another folder — note it. Update that folder's context too.
