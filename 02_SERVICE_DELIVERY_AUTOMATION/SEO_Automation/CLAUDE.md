# 02/SEO_Automation — Working guide

Plans for the SEO engine serving **SEO retainer clients**: rank tracking, content scheduling,
technical-SEO execution, and monthly reports. `CONTEXT.md` = inventory; this file = how to work here.

## How this connects to FMOS / the rest
- The `SEO` service here is what clients buy — mirrored in the proposal builder (`01_CRM_AND_TOOL/fmos/lib/data/services_data.json`) and priced in `08_FINANCE/Pricing_Decisions` (Starter/Growth/Dominate tiers). Keep tiers in sync with Finance + the bot's `pricing.md`.
- Keyword/competitor inputs come from `07_DATA_AND_RESEARCH` (Keyword_Data, Competitor_Data).
- Monthly SEO reports feed `04_CLIENT_MANAGEMENT/Monthly_Reports`; content tasks run through FMOS tasks/projects.
- Don't confuse with `05_FORTUNEMARQ_ONLINE_PRESENCE/SEO_and_Local_SEO`, which is FortuneMarq's *own* SEO.

## Boundaries
- Planning docs only. No client login data or secrets.
