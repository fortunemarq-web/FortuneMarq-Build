# FortuneMarq — Payment & Cancellation Policy
**Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 2026

---

## Payment Policy

**Invoice Schedule**
- Monthly invoices are raised on the **1st of every month**.
- Payment is due by the **5th of every month**.

**Accepted Payment Methods**
- Bank transfer (account details on invoice)
- UPI / Google Pay / PhonePe
- Cash (office only — with receipt)

**Overdue Payments**
- **7 days overdue:** All Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns are paused immediately. The client is notified on the day of pausing via WhatsApp.
- **30 days overdue:** The client's website is taken offline. The client is notified 3 days before this happens.
- **Service resumption:** All paused services resume the same day payment is confirmed. No extra charges for reinstatement.

**Setup Fees**
- All setup fees are due **before work begins**.
- No work starts — no brief form, no account creation, no campaign setup — until the setup fee invoice is settled.

---

## Cancellation Policy

**Exit Notice**
- Either the client or FortuneMarq can exit the agreement with **30 days written notice** via WhatsApp or email.
- Notice is counted from the date the message is sent and acknowledged.

**Fees on Exit**
- Monthly fees are **pro-rated** — the client pays only for days of active service in the final month.
- If the client owes any outstanding amount, it must be settled within 7 days of the exit date.

**Asset Handover on Exit**
- All accounts, credentials, and digital assets created for the client are the client's property.
- FortuneMarq will hand over all assets within **7 working days** of the final invoice being settled.
- FortuneMarq will remove its own access from all client accounts within **3 working days** of asset handover.

**What Gets Handed Over**
| Asset | Details |
|---|---|
| Website | Full files or Antigravity project link handed over |
| GMB | FortuneMarq removed from profile managers |
| Google Ads | FortuneMarq removed from account. Campaign history stays. |
| Meta Ads | FortuneMarq removed from Business Manager. Campaign history stays. |
| SEO | All on-page work remains on the website. GSC and Analytics access removed. |
| WhatsApp Marketing | Message template exports + contact list CSV handed over |
| AI Automations | Documentation + workflow files handed over. Third-party access transferred. |

## Late Payment Communication Flow (Jabeer's SOP)

1. **5th of month — no payment received:**
   FMOS flags the invoice as overdue. Jabeer sends a WhatsApp reminder.

2. **7th of month — still unpaid:**
   Jabeer pauses ad campaigns (Google / Meta). Sends WhatsApp: *"Hi [Name], I've had to pause your campaigns today as the invoice is overdue. Please confirm payment to resume — I'll get them back on the same day."*

3. **20th of month — still unpaid:**
   Jabeer calls the client directly. Logs outcome in FMOS.

4. **30th of month — still unpaid:**
   Jabeer sends a 3-day warning via WhatsApp: *"Hi [Name], if the invoice is not settled by [date], we will need to take the website offline temporarily. Please get in touch so we can sort this."*

5. **30 days + 3 days — unpaid:**
   Website taken offline. FMOS logs action. Client status changed to AT-RISK.

6. **Payment received at any stage:**
   Services reinstated same day. Invoice marked as paid in FMOS. Client status normalised.
