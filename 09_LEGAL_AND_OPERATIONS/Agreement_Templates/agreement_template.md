## FMOS GENERATION NOTES

**Trigger:** Generated from FMOS after client verbally confirms proposal
**Delivery:** Jabeer sends manually via WhatsApp or email
**Confirmation:** Client replies "Yes, confirmed" — Jabeer logs this in FMOS and changes lead status to CLIENT
**What FMOS auto-fills:**
- agreementNumber (auto-generated: AGR-YYYY-NNN)
- proposalNumber (linked from the proposal)
- agreementDate (today's date)
- businessName, ownerName, city (from lead profile)
- All service rows and totals (from the linked proposal)
- startDate (Jabeer selects when generating)
