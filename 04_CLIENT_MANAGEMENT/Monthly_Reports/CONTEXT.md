# L6 — Monthly Reports + Client Health Score
**Created:** April 2026 | **Status:** COMPLETE

## Purpose
Monthly performance reports generated as PDFs and sent to every active client. Covers Google rankings, website traffic, leads generated, and GMB insights. Each report includes a Client Health Score (0–100) to track account health over time.

## Files
| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `FMOS_Report_Data/monthly_report_schema.json` | Full report structure — all sections, fields, data sources |
| `FMOS_Report_Data/client_health_score.json` | Health score rules — 4 pillars, scoring logic, tiers, actions |
| `FMOS_Report_Data/report_index.ts` | TypeScript loader + health score calculator function |

## Report Sections
1. Cover Page — client info, report month, health score + tier
2. Google Rankings — keyword positions, changes, top 10 count
3. Website Traffic — visits, sources, top pages, MoM change
4. Leads Generated — calls, forms, WhatsApp enquiries, total
5. GMB Insights — profile views, calls, direction requests, reviews
6. What We Did This Month — deliverables and work summary
7. Plan for Next Month — upcoming tasks and focus
8. Health Score Breakdown — pillar scores and tier explanation

## Health Score System
- **4 pillars:** Rankings (25) + Traffic (25) + Leads (25) + GMB (25) = 100 total
- **Performance-only** — no subjective factors
- **Tiers:** Excellent (80–100) | Good (60–79) | Needs Attention (40–59) | At Risk (0–39)
- **New client base score:** 10 points per pillar for first 2 months (rankings take 60–90 days)
- **Upsell trigger:** Excellent tier for 2 consecutive months → flag for upsell conversation

## Delivery
- Format: PDF
- Generated by: FMOS (to be built in Phase C/D)
- Sent by: Jabeer manually after generation
- Frequency: Monthly, within first 3 days of each new month
