# 04 — Monthly Reports
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-28 (revised: FMOS production-ready v4.5) | **Status:** COMPLETE — monthly_report_schema.json + client_health_score.json + report_index.ts created

## Folder Purpose
Monthly performance reports generated as PDFs and sent to every active client. Covers Google rankings, website traffic, leads generated, and GMB insights. Each report includes a Client Health Score (0–100) to track account health over time. Health score also triggers upsell conversations.

## Report Structure (8 Sections)
1. **Cover Page** — Client name, business logo space, report month, health score + tier badge, prepared by FortuneMarq
2. **Google Rankings** — Keyword positions, month-over-month changes, top 10 keyword count, trending up/down
3. **Website Traffic** — Total visits, traffic sources breakdown, top pages, MoM change percentage
4. **Leads Generated** — Phone calls, contact form fills, WhatsApp enquiries, total leads
5. **GMB Insights** — Profile views, calls from GMB, direction requests, photo views, review count + rating
6. **What We Did This Month** — Deliverables completed, work summary, milestones hit
7. **Plan for Next Month** — Upcoming tasks, focus areas, scheduled work
8. **Health Score Breakdown** — Per-pillar scores, tier explanation, what it means for them

## Health Score System
- **4 pillars:** Rankings (25 pts) + Traffic (25 pts) + Leads (25 pts) + GMB (25 pts) = 100 total
- **Performance-only** — no subjective factors (payment, communication)
- **Tiers:**
  - Excellent: 80–100 (growing well, flag for upsell)
  - Good: 60–79 (healthy, maintain)
  - Needs Attention: 40–59 (underperforming, investigate)
  - At Risk: 0–39 (at risk of churn, retention call from Jabeer)
- **New client base score:** 10 points per pillar for first 2 months (results take 60–90 days to show)
- **Upsell trigger:** Excellent tier for 2 consecutive months → FMOS surfaces client in upsell queue

## Report Delivery
- Format: PDF (generated by FMOS — Phase D/E build)
- Frequency: Monthly, within first 3 days of each new month
- Sent by: Jabeer manually via WhatsApp after generating in FMOS
- Storage: FMOS client profile → Reports tab → archived by month
