# FortuneMarq — Client Onboarding SOP
**Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** April 2026
**Owner:** Jabeer

---

## What Is This?

This SOP covers everything that happens from the moment a client confirms the agreement to the moment their services are live and running. Every step is tracked in FMOS. No work starts before the setup fee is paid.

---

## The Golden Rules of Onboarding

1. **No work starts before setup fee payment is confirmed.** Not even the brief form.
2. **Every asset goes into the Asset Vault in FMOS.** Never stored in personal WhatsApp, laptop, or email only.
3. **Never start a build task without a complete brief.** Incomplete briefs cause rework.
4. **Client communication is always friendly and clear.** No jargon. WhatsApp is the primary channel.
5. **FMOS is the record of truth.** If it's not logged in FMOS, it didn't happen.

## Step-by-Step Onboarding Flow

### STEP 1 — Agreement Confirmed
**Who:** Jabeer
**When:** Client replies "Yes, confirmed" to the agreement message

- Go to FMOS → Lead profile → Change status from **PROPOSAL SENT** to **CLIENT**
- Change deal stage to **AGREEMENT CONFIRMED**
- Log the client's confirmation reply (screenshot or copy-paste into FMOS notes)
- FMOS auto-creates the onboarding task list for all services signed

---

### STEP 2 — Raise the Setup Fee Invoice
**Who:** Jabeer
**When:** Same day as agreement confirmation

- Go to FMOS → Client profile → Finance → Create Invoice
- Invoice type: **Setup Fee**
- Amount: Total setup fee from agreement
- Due: **Immediately** (no days — advance payment)
- Send via WhatsApp (FMOS generates the invoice PDF)
- Log invoice number in FMOS

> **Message to send with invoice:**
> *"Hi [Name], great to have you on board! Here's your setup invoice — once this is cleared we'll get started right away. Payment details are on the invoice. Let me know if you have any questions."*

---

### STEP 3 — Wait for Setup Fee Payment
**Who:** Jabeer
**When:** After invoice is sent

- Follow up once if not paid within 24 hours
- **DO NOT start any work until payment is confirmed**
- Once payment received: mark invoice as PAID in FMOS, log payment date

---

### STEP 4 — Send Welcome Message
**Who:** Jabeer
**When:** Same day payment is confirmed

Use WhatsApp template: **ONBOARD_WELCOME**

> *"Hi [Name]! Payment received — thank you. We're officially getting started on your [services]. I'll be reaching out shortly to collect a few things we need from you. This will be quick — most of it just takes a few minutes on your end. Looking forward to this!"*

---

### STEP 5 — Asset Collection
**Who:** Jabeer
**When:** Within 24–48 hours of welcome message

- Open FMOS → Client profile → Onboarding → Asset Checklist
- Work through the checklist for each service the client signed for
- Collect assets via WhatsApp (phone friendly — most clients will just send photos and text)
- Store every asset in FMOS → Asset Vault immediately after receiving
- **Never leave assets only in your WhatsApp chat**

**Tips for collecting assets smoothly:**
- Send a short WhatsApp voice note or message explaining what you need and why — clients respond better when they understand the purpose
- For logos: *"Can you send me your logo? A PNG file is best. If you don't have it saved, your designer or printer would have it — or even your letterhead PDF works."*
- For photos: *"Can you take 10–15 photos of your [business type]? Inside, outside, and your [service/products]. Normal phone camera is perfect."*
- For domain/hosting: *"I'll need the login for where your website is registered. This is usually GoDaddy, Hostinger, or similar. Just the email and password for that account."*

---

### STEP 6 — Assign Build Tasks (Website Only)
**Who:** Jabeer
**When:** After Website Brief Form is complete and reviewed

- Review the completed Brief Form in FMOS
- Create a website build task in FMOS
- Assign to an outsourced freelance builder based on availability
- Include: Brief Form link, Antigravity access details, client photos folder link
- Set deadline: Stage 1 preview in 4–5 days

**Brief Form must be 100% complete before assigning. No exceptions.**

---

### STEP 7 — Monitor and Chase
**Who:** Jabeer
**When:** Throughout onboarding period

- Check FMOS onboarding task list daily
- If a task is overdue by 1 day — follow up via WhatsApp
- If assets are delayed by client — send a gentle reminder:
  *"Hi [Name], just following up on the [asset]. Once I have it we can move forward quickly — do let me know if you need any help getting it together."*
- If a freelancer misses a deadline — message them directly, log the delay in FMOS

---

### STEP 8 — Final Review Before Go-Live (Website Only)
**Who:** Jabeer
**When:** After the freelancer submits for final review

Check:
- All pages present and correct
- Mobile responsive
- All forms working (contact form, WhatsApp button)
- Speed acceptable (basic check on PageSpeed Insights)
- Content matches the brief
- No placeholder text or images left

If approved → send preview link to client for confirmation.
**Get written client approval before going live.** Log approval in FMOS.

---

### STEP 10 — Mark Onboarding Complete
**Who:** Jabeer
**When:** All tasks done and all required assets stored

- FMOS → Client profile → Onboarding → Mark as COMPLETE
- Client status changes to: **ACTIVE**
- Health Score is initialised (starts at default score)
- Monthly report cycle begins (first report in ~30 days)
- Recurring invoice schedule activated in FMOS (1st of next month)

## Common Onboarding Problems — and How to Handle Them

**Client takes days to send assets**
→ Send one reminder after 24 hours. Call after 48 hours. Frame it as helping them, not chasing them: *"The faster I get these, the sooner we can go live for you."*

**Client sends wrong/low-quality logo**
→ Ask: *"Do you have this in a higher resolution? Even your WhatsApp business profile picture works if it's the clearest version you have."* Do not reject — work with what you get.

**Client has no photos**
→ Tell them what to take on their phone. Most clients will do this within a day if given clear instructions.

**Domain login is wrong**
→ Do a screen share call with the client and sort it together. Log that this was resolved in FMOS.

**Freelancer build doesn't match brief**
→ Don't show it to client. Reject internally in FMOS with specific feedback. Ask the freelancer to redo.

**WhatsApp API approval delayed**
→ Normal — can take 3–5 days sometimes. Keep client informed. Meanwhile, proceed with template writing and contact list collection.
