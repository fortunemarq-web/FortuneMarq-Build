## Summary

FortuneMarq's Phase 1 targets six priority niches in Hubli-Dharwad, selected based on real Google Keyword Planner search volume data. These niches have high monthly search volumes with very few competitors actively running digital ads or ranking organically — creating a strong opportunity gap.

## Priority Niches (Hubli)

| Niche | Monthly Searches | Opportunity |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| Gyms | 63,950 | Only 3 websites getting traffic |
| Skin Clinics | 41,850 | Top clinic gets only 173 visits — 99% traffic gap |
| Computer Training | 24,350 | High search, low digital presence |
| Dental Clinics | 21,100 | Strong intent, underserved online |
| Car Rentals | 16,450 | Zero paid ads running |
| JEE/NEET Coaching | 12,300 | High-value, strong conversion intent |

**Total across all 6 niches: ~180,000 monthly searches in Hubli alone.**

## Key Market Insight

70% of search traffic goes to directories (JustDial, Sulekha, Practo). FortuneMarq's value proposition: bypass directories and get clients direct calls from Google, not directory listings. This is the core sales angle used in all outreach.

## Data Hook (use in all sales conversations)

- "Zero paid ads running in all 6 niches — your competitors aren't advertising. First mover wins."
- "63,950 people search for gyms in Hubli every month. 3 websites capture it all."
- "Your top competitor's website gets 173 visits/month from 41,850 searches. That's 99% of traffic going to waste."

## Lead Script Variants (4 types based on SERP data)

- **Type A:** Lead already ranked on Google — reinforce and expand
- **Type B:** Lead has website but not ranking — fix what's broken
- **Type C:** Lead has no website — start from scratch
- **Type D:** Low-volume niche — different value angle

## Expansion Plan

Phase 1 is Hubli-Dharwad only. Pipeline for other cities (Belgaum, Mangalore, Davangere, Ballari) is ready to run. Mysuru, Kalaburgi, Vijayapura need SERP collection first.
