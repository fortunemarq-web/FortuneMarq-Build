## Summary

FortuneMarq's brand is data-driven, transparent, and systems-focused. Every piece of content and communication leads with real numbers. The tone is confident but not arrogant — backed by proof, not promises.

## Brand Identity

- **Name:** FortuneMarq
- **Tagline:** "Marketing That Pays You Back"
- **Primary color:** #42CA80 (accent green)
- **UI palette:** Slate grays (SaaS Light theme, Tailwind CSS)
- **Logo variants:** 3 versions — white background, black background, green background
- **Fonts:** JetBrains Mono (Regular), Degarism Alliance (2 variants)

## Voice & Tone

- **Lead with data.** Every claim is backed by a real number from Google Keyword Planner or SERP analysis.
- **Be specific.** Not "many businesses" but "63,950 people search for gyms in Hubli every month."
- **Be direct.** No fluff, no motivational language. State the problem, state the opportunity, state what FortuneMarq does about it.
- **Transparent.** Show the gap, explain why it exists, explain what FortuneMarq will do.
- **Systems-thinker language.** Talk about "the machine", "the pipeline", "the system" — not just individual tactics.

## Core Sales Narrative

The market has a massive digital gap. Thousands of local searchers can't find local businesses because those businesses don't have digital presence. Directories (JustDial, Sulekha, Practo) capture 70% of traffic. FortuneMarq bypasses directories and puts the client directly in front of searchers — getting them direct calls, not directory traffic.

## Signature Data Hooks (use in all outreach)

- "Zero paid ads running in 6 priority niches in Hubli"
- "63,950 people search for gyms in Hubli every month. Only 3 websites capture that traffic."
- "Your top competitor's skin clinic gets 173 visits/month from 41,850 searches. 99% traffic gap."
- "Directory dominance angle: 70% of search traffic goes to JustDial/Sulekha/Practo. We bypass that."

## What NOT to Do

- Don't make vague promises ("we'll grow your business")
- Don't use generic marketing language ("full-service agency", "360° solutions")
- Don't claim guarantees (not offered in Phase 1 — see [[LockedDecisions]])
