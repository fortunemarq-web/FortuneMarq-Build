## Current Status (April 2026)

Company MRR is ₹0 — the business has not yet landed its first client. The build phase is ~90% complete. The primary blocker is deploying FMOS to production, which gates the entire outbound sales operation. See [[CurrentBlockers]] for the full picture.

## The Core Machine

Every part of the business is interconnected. The master flow runs: data exists → campaign runs → lead captured in FMOS → 3-touch outreach (Afifa) → meeting (Jabeer) → proposal → agreement → invoice → onboarding → delivery → monthly report → health score → upsell → renewal.

The business is built across 11 system folders: 00_MASTER through 10_PERSONAL_GROWTH. Each folder has a CONTEXT.md that tracks decisions and session history.

## What Makes FortuneMarq Different

- All pricing is backed by real keyword search data (Google Keyword Planner)
- 8,000 Hubli leads already scraped and cleaned before the first call
- 75 market intelligence PDFs generated and ready to send as conversation starters
- Custom CRM (FMOS) built from scratch — no spreadsheets, no external tools
- Automation handles repeatable work; humans only supervise and close
