## Summary

FortuneMarq offers digital marketing services priced for accessibility, with a volume model that upsells retainers over time. All pricing is locked — do not re-discuss unless Jabeer initiates a review.

## Key Policy

Client always pays for their own ad spend (Google/Meta budget). FortuneMarq charges a management fee only — it does not bundle ad spend into service fees.

## Payment Policy (Locked)

- Invoice issued: 1st of month
- Due: 5th of month
- 7 days overdue: Campaigns paused + WhatsApp alert to client
- 30 days overdue (website clients): Website taken offline until paid

## Upsell Paths

The natural progression for most clients:
- GMB → Google Ads (trigger: 50+ GMB calls/month)
- Website → GMB (trigger: website go-live)
- Google Ads → Meta Ads (trigger: after 3 months of Google results)
- Any retainer → WhatsApp Marketing (trigger: client wants lead nurturing)
