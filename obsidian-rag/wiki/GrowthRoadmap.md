## Summary

FortuneMarq's growth targets are defined in clear monthly milestones. The model is volume-first: land many clients at accessible prices, upsell retainers over time, and hire only after revenue justifies it.

## Revenue Milestones

| Milestone | Target | Key Requirement |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| End of April/May 2026 | ₹50,000 MRR | FMOS live + Afifa calling + first clients signed |
| Month 4–5 | ₹1,00,000 MRR | Multiple niches active, upsells beginning |
| Month 7–8 | ₹2,00,000 MRR | Hiring trigger — first non-founder hire |
| Year 2 | ₹5,00,000 MRR | 5–10 person team + SaaS product live |

## What ₹50K MRR Looks Like

At current pricing, ₹50,000 MRR could come from combinations like:
- ~14 × GMB clients at ₹3,500/month
- 10 × Ads Management at ₹2,500/month + 8 × GMB at ₹3,500/month
- Mix of retainers across niches

The key is retainer stacking — each client ideally holds 2–3 monthly services.

## Phase Structure

**Phase 1 (Now — Month 3):** Hubli-Dharwad only. All 6 niches. 3-touch outreach. First clients. First case studies.

**Phase 2 (Month 4+):** Expand to Belgaum, Mangalore, Davangere, Ballari using the same data pipeline. Price review for new clients.

**Phase 3 (Year 2):** SaaS product — FMOS as a product sold to other agencies. National expansion.

## Monthly Burn
- Current burn: ₹15,600–16,600/month (infrastructure, tools, minimal overhead)
- Personal freelance income (₹15–20K) covers burn while company ramps
