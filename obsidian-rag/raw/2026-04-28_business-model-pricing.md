# Business Model & Pricing

## Core Model
Volume-first: accessible prices → fast delivery via automation → strong portfolio → price increases over time. Retainer revenue is the goal; one-time projects build the portfolio and create upsell paths.

## Service Pricing (Locked)

| Service | Type | Price |
|---------|------|-------|
| Landing Page | One-time | ₹5K–₹8K |
| Website (basic) | One-time | ₹8K–₹20K |
| Google My Business (GMB) | Monthly retainer | ₹2,500/month |
| Google Ads Setup | One-time | ₹4,500 |
| Google Ads Management | Monthly retainer | ₹2,500/month |
| Meta Ads Management | Monthly retainer | ₹2,500/month |
| SEO | Monthly retainer | ₹7K–₹15K+/month |
| WhatsApp Marketing Setup | One-time + retainer | ₹5K + ₹2,500/month |
| AI Automations | Custom | Custom pricing |

Note: Client always pays for their own ad spend. FortuneMarq charges management fee only.

## Payment Policy (Locked)
- Invoice issued: 1st of every month
- Payment due: 5th of every month
- 7 days overdue: Campaigns paused + WhatsApp alert sent to client
- 30 days overdue (website clients): Website taken offline until payment received

## Phase 1 Geography
Hubli-Dharwad ONLY until all niches are called and campaigned. No expansion until Phase 1 is fully executed.

## Lead Flow (Master Flow)
Data exists → Campaign runs → Lead captured in FMOS → 3-touch outreach (Afifa) → Meeting (Jabeer) → Proposal → Agreement → Invoice → Onboarding → Delivery (cousins) → Monthly report → Health score → Upsell → Renewal
