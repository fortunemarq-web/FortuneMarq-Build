# 00_MASTER/Brand_Assets — Working instructions for Claude

Canonical brand assets: logos, fonts, and the brand guidelines image. These are the **source files**
the marketing site, LPs, proposals, and reports draw their look from.

## How to work here
- Treat these as the authoritative originals. Fonts here (Alliance No1/No2, JetBrains Mono) and the green palette must match what the app uses — brand green = `brand-deep #1E7A4F` for text/buttons, raw `#42CA80` for accents/fills only.
- If a design needs an asset, reference/copy from here — don't recreate or re-color logos.
- Binary assets (PNG/JPG/TTF/OTF/WOFF2) are not editable as text; swap the file, don't try to patch it.

## Boundaries
- Don't delete or rename existing logo/font files without owner approval — they're referenced across the app and docs.
- No app source or secrets here.
