**The core idea (use this often):** Thousands of people search for local businesses on Google every month, but most of that traffic goes to directories (JustDial, Sulekha, Practo, MagicBricks) instead of the businesses themselves. We build a direct path from Google to the business — so the owner gets the calls, not a directory.

**How we're different:** we lead with real data about the business's own market (search volumes, where the traffic is going), we build complete systems (not one-off services), and we keep prices accessible.
